Do I need to have a bet running already?

No. Radar is built around existing arbitrage opportunities on upcoming matches. You can find an opportunity and prepare all of its stakes before placing your first bet.

Does Dycers place bets for me?

No. Dycers compares odds, calculates stakes and helps you track your bets. You place each bet directly with the relevant bookmaker; saving a plan in the app does not place it.

Is the displayed return guaranteed?

The calculation uses the displayed odds and stakes. Real outcomes depend on obtaining those prices, having every stake accepted and the bookmakers’ settlement rules. Odds can move, and betting involves financial risk.

Can I still follow live matches?

Yes. My bets includes live and upcoming views for your saved bets, with match scores where available. Tracking remains part of the app, alongside the focus on finding opportunities before kick-off.

Is the app free?

Dycers is free to download on iOS and Android. A free plan is available, with optional paid plans for additional access. You can see the current plan details in the app.
